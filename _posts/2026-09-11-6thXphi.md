## Attending and presenting Poster at the 6th European Experimental Philosophy Conference
---

<div class="row align-items-start">
<div class="col-md-4">
<p>The <a href="https://sites.unica.it/6euroxphi/">6th edition of the X-Phi Conference</a> took place in Cagliari last week. It was hosted by the University of Cagliari from the 8th to the 11th September.</p>
<p>I had the opportunity to present my research on the interspecies relationship between humans and animals in poster format. You can take a closer look at the poster on the right.</p>
<p>This research forms part of a larger project called <em>The Animal Dilemmas Project</em>, in which we investigate how people perceive non-human moral entities in the context of dilemma-based decision-making.</p>
<p>For this poster presentation, I developed a mobile-friendly model of the dilemma-based trials that participants undertook. You can find it <a href="https://k1rjix7m0q.cognition.run">here</a>.</p>
