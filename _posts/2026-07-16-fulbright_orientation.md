## Three days orientation sessions for the next year Fulbright grantees

Last week, I had the pleasure of attending the <a href="https://fulbright.org/">Fulbright Association</a> Orientation Days in Madrid.

I'm one of the selected grantees for the 2026-2027 cohort, and I'll be continuing my PhD research in the US next year. My work on Reasonableness will continue at <a href="https://www.law.georgetown.edu/">Georgetown Law</a>, under the supervision of Professor <a href="https://www.law.georgetown.edu/faculty/kevin-tobia/">Kevin Tobia</a>.

A huge thank you to the <a href="https://fulbright.es/">Fulbright Spain</a> for organizing such structured, fun, and collaborative sessions. With around 80 new Fulbrighters in this cohort, from predoctoral researchers to teaching assistants, graduates, and artists, it would have been easy to feel lost in the crowd. But the team made a genuine effort to foster real connections, not just based on where we're headed, but also on our academic backgrounds and the type of grant we each have. That personal touch made a big difference, and I think it'll help a lot once we're all spread across the U.S.

It's a real pleasure to become part of this community. Looking forward to meeting everyone again on the other side of the Atlantic! 🦅🦅
